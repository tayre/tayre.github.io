A spectral remainder in the Hellinger-to-information implication

Read the paper (PDF)

A potentially new quantitative refinement. Assuming the cited Hellinger inequality, the information deficit is at least one sixth of the difference between the squared noise correlation and the variance of the noisy Boolean function. This gives a quantitative bound on distance to a signed coordinate. A separate corollary applies to antipodal functions using Ky's Hellinger theorem.

This is a research note. Novelty and publication priority remain unconfirmed.
