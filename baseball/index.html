    Double plays, sacrifices, and third outs

    Double play: tap fielders in order, then mark both retired players Out. Sacrifices: choose Other, then tap Sacrifice fly or Sacrifice bunt.

    Third out: runs don’t count on a force out or when the batter is retired before first. On timing plays, select Home only for runners who crossed before the out; leave others at their last base. Check “Count runs” to include them.

    This scorebook tracks runs, hits, errors, outs, and batting order. It doesn’t calculate RBI or earned runs.
