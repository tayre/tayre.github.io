A sharp spectral-gap refinement for the switch chain

Read the paper (PDF)

For simple undirected graphs with a fixed degree sequence, let L be the unnormalized Laplacian of the graph of realizations, with each distinct valid switch neighbor counted once. The proposed bound is λ2(L) ≥ 2 whenever there is more than one realization. The constant 2 is sharp. For the source's lazy switch chain, the resulting mixing-time guarantee improves from O(n8) to O(n6).

Status: a potentially novel quantitative refinement, conditional on two lemmas from OpenAI's source manuscript. The new step strengthens its disjoint-pair estimate to D ≥ H. The closest located sharp analogue concerns bipartite graphs. Novelty remains unconfirmed.
