The question behind the numbers

Simple to state.
Still open.

A tree is a connected graph with no cycles. For a tree with n vertices, assign the numbers 0 through n − 1 to the vertices, using each number once. Then take the absolute difference of the labels at the ends of every edge.

If those differences are exactly 1 through n − 1, the labeling is graceful. The Graceful Tree Conjecture says every finite tree has at least one such labeling.

Fang’s 2010 computational study reports verification for every tree through 35 vertices. Our small exhaustive checks validate this software; they do not improve that published bound. Read the paper ↗

The research goal

Build reliable evidence. Understand difficult cases. Look for precisely stated properties that could become useful mathematics.

The long-term ambition is to discover a counterexample or contribute to understanding why trees are graceful. Computation can suggest where to look; a universal claim still needs a proof.
01 / Generate

Explore different shapes.

Enumerate non-isomorphic small trees and sample larger trees from reproducible families. Exact tree isomorphism checks avoid counting the same shape twice.

02 / Search & check

Keep the evidence.

OR-Tools CP-SAT searches for labelings. A separate verifier checks the graph, every vertex label, and every edge difference. Inputs, seeds, versions, and witnesses are saved.

03 / Learn & repeat

Make the next run useful.

Resume the cumulative ledger, retry unresolved cases, and investigate where the largest edge difference can occur. Deterministic rules propose follow-up experiments for human review.

How to read a result

Verified graceful
A checked labeling proves this particular tree is graceful.
Unresolved / timed out
The search did not settle the question. No negative mathematical conclusion follows.
Solver infeasible
The solver reports no solution for its model. This version cannot independently certify that negative claim.

The next questions

From observation to experiment.

These are proposed investigations, not established new results. Truth and novelty require separate review.

Candidate question

Within a specified tree family, can the edge with the largest difference always touch a leaf?

Test every relevant edge orbit and keep positive witnesses. Check the prescribed-labeling literature before claiming novelty.
