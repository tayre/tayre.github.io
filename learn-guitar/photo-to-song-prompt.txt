Format rules:
- id: lowercase letters/numbers separated by hyphens; title: printed song title.
- tempo: 40–200 quarter-note BPM. Use a gentle practice tempo if none is printed,
  and mention that choice in source.description.
- beatsPerBar: 1–6 quarter beats, for simple x/4 meters. Preserve the sheet's meter.
- tuning: six MIDI pitches from THIN high e to THICK low E. Standard is
  [64,59,55,50,45,40]. capo: 0–12. Frets are relative to the capo; the player
  adds the capo pitch offset. Label any assumed standard tuning/no capo.
- rawBars: one array per bar: [notesOrRepeat, optionalChordLabel, optionalHint].
- A note is string:fret:duration. Strings 1–6 run high e to low E, frets 0–24;
  0 is open. Duration is in quarter beats: .5 eighth, 1 quarter, 1.5 dotted
  quarter, 2 half, 3 dotted half, 4 whole. Separate notes with spaces.
- r:duration is a rest. string:fret:duration:t continues the immediately
  preceding identical string/fret without picking again, including across bars.
- Every bar must total beatsPerBar. Durations must be positive half-beat
  increments. Do not pad a pickup bar or shorten a note just to pass validation.
- A numeric notesOrRepeat copies an earlier ONE-BASED bar's melody. Only use
  exact matches of strings, frets, rhythm, rests, and ties. Chord labels can differ.
  The importer also detects exact repeats, so explicit notes are acceptable.
- Expand repeat signs into playback order, including alternate endings, and
  explain that expansion in source.description. Do not mistake slurs for ties:
  hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, and bends are not supported yet.
- Chord labels appear above the tab. They do not generate chord audio. Add simple
  suggested chords for a parent to strum along, and label them as suggestions in
  accompaniment. If two chords share a bar, describe when to change. Keep printed
  chords when present. Do not call suggested chords part of the source.
- chordShapes use [lowE,A,D,G,B,highE], the opposite order from tuning. -1 muted,
  0 open, frets 1–4 supported. Omit a diagram that needs a higher position.
- sections use inclusive one-based start/end bars. tips are [heading, body]
  pairs. Keep hints short and useful for a beginner. repeatSummary is optional;
  the importer fills it automatically when omitted.
- source.description records the source and any interpretation. Omit source.image;
  the import tool attaches the photo. No absolute paths or remote image URLs.
- transcription.rhythm: "from-photo", "interpreted", or "user-specified".
  Use "interpreted" when the rhythm is inferred rather than clearly specified.
- transcription.chords: "from-photo", "suggested", or "none".
- transcription.unresolved: an array of specific questions; empty only after
  resolving them. The importer refuses to publish an unresolved transcription.
- This version supports six-string, single-note tab in simple x/4 meters with
  half-beat increments. Triplets, sixteenth notes, compound meters, simultaneous
  notes, mixed meters, pickup bars, and playing techniques need a player extension
  or an explicitly agreed simplification. Report them instead of losing detail.
