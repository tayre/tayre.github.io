Tab help, repeat hints & chords

Lines are strings. Thin high e is at the top; thick low E is at the bottom. Read left to right, then move to the next line.

Numbers are frets. 0 = an open string. A small line means let it ring; an arc joins tied notes—don’t pick the second one.

Follow the count. “&” is halfway between beats. “rest” means stop playing. A small “= 1” beside a bar number means its melody repeats bar 1.

Play together

Let one guitar take the melody. On the second, try a gentle down-strum at the beginning of each bar.

Shapes run from thick E → thin e. × = skip; ○ = open.
