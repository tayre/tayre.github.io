Tab help, rhythm & repeat hints

Tap the sheet to play or pause. Tap again to resume from the same spot. Scrolling and the controls won’t toggle playback.

Lines are strings. Thin high e is at the top; thick low E is at the bottom. Read left to right, then move to the next line.

Numbers are frets. 0 = an open string. A small line means let it ring; an arc joins tied notes—don’t pick the second one.

Follow the rhythm. The notes below the strings show how long to play. Flagged or joined eighth notes last half a beat each; “&” is halfway between counts. Rest symbols mean silence.

Note lengths: filled note + stem = 1 beat; hollow note + stem = 2; hollow note without a stem = 4. A dot adds half the note’s value. Curved ties join the lengths without another pick. “= 1” beside a bar number means the melody repeats bar 1.

Play together

Let one guitar take the melody. On the second, try a gentle down-strum at the beginning of each bar.

Shapes run from thick E → thin e. × = skip; ○ = open.
