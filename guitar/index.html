01

The root names the chord.

C is the root of C major and C minor. It’s the note the chord is built around, not necessarily the highest note you hear.

02

The third changes the character.

A major third sits four semitones above the root. Lower it one semitone for a minor third. Keep the root and fifth the same.

03

A shape is one way to play it.

The same chord can appear all over the neck. Its notes may change order or octave; the ingredients still give it its identity.
