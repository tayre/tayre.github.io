    MADE FOR THE IN-BETWEEN

    A quieter way to fly.

    Still breaks your flight into small, manageable chapters. Progress uses your takeoff time and planned duration. It doesn’t confirm takeoff, landing, or your actual route.

    Once “Ready for offline” appears, the page and quiet moments are saved on this device. Open it before boarding. Weather and flight details load when you open or switch journeys, and when you refresh them. No background polling runs. Your timer and calming exercises work offline.

    A little honesty about data

    Weather shows a travel-day forecast near the destination airport, or the hour closest to your arrival when a reported or estimated arrival time is available. It cannot predict turbulence or landing conditions for a particular aircraft.
