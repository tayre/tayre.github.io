A note on the air

Night can bring less surface-heating turbulence near the ground. Conditions at cruising altitude are different, so night alone doesn’t promise a smoother ride.

Public forecasts can’t tell us exactly when your bumps will end. The crew has the most relevant information for your flight.

MADE FOR THE IN-BETWEEN

A quieter way to fly.

Still breaks your flight into small, manageable chapters. Progress uses your takeoff time and planned duration. It doesn’t confirm takeoff, landing, or your actual route.

Once “Ready for offline” appears, the page and quiet moments are saved on this device. Open it before boarding. Weather uses the internet only when you request it. Your timer and calming exercises work offline.

A little honesty about data

Arrival weather is a model forecast near the destination airport, for the hour closest to your estimated arrival. It cannot predict turbulence or landing conditions for a particular aircraft.
