About the data

Public AIS from the Norwegian Coastal Administration, filtered to MMSI 258465000. The marker is the last reported position, never an estimated position.

The ship drawing is illustrative; its size and direction are not to scale. The teal dot marks the reported coordinates. The Arctic Circle is drawn at approximately 66.56° N.

Distances are approximate great-circle distances over the Earth, rounded to the nearest kilometre. Guelph uses the supplied address’s coordinates from OpenStreetMap; the North Pole is geographic north at 90° N. These are not sailing or driving distances.

The thin line is the short track supplied by the feed, when available. The feed retains recent positions for up to 20 minutes; this is not a 24-hour history.

Reports can be delayed or missing outside Norwegian AIS coverage. If updates fail, the last successful report stays visible with its original time. Refreshing does not change the report time.

Checks run every minute while this tab is visible and online. The destination is the ship’s transmitted text. Times are UTC.
