About the data

Public AIS from the Norwegian Coastal Administration. MS Trollfjord is identified by MMSI 258465000. Muted markers show other reported vessels within 50 miles (80.5 km), excluding reports over 20 minutes old. Select a vessel for details. Markers show reported positions.

The ship drawing is illustrative; its size and direction are not to scale. The teal dot marks the reported coordinates. The Arctic Circle is drawn at approximately 66.56° N.

Distances are approximate great-circle distances over the Earth, rounded to the nearest kilometre. Guelph uses the supplied address’s coordinates from OpenStreetMap; the North Pole is geographic north at 90° N. Arctic Circle distance is measured north or south to 66.56° N at the ship’s longitude. These are not sailing or driving distances.

Speed is reported in knots and converted using 1 kn = 1.852 km/h. Very small speeds while moored may reflect AIS position noise. Speed over ground differs from speed through the water.

Air temperature is a model estimate at 2 m above the surface from Open-Meteo’s weather models. Its report time is UTC. Air temperature and sun times update every 15 minutes or after the ship moves 5 km, using its latest AIS position. Sunrise and sunset are calculated for that location and shown in its local time zone for the displayed date. Polar day and polar night are shown when the sun does not set or rise.

The thin line is the short track supplied by the feed, when available. The feed retains recent positions for up to 20 minutes; this is not a 24-hour history.

Reports can be delayed or missing outside Norwegian AIS coverage. If updates fail, the last successful report stays visible with its original time. Refreshing does not change the report time.

Checks run every 10 seconds while this tab is visible and online, updating the position without reloading the page. The destination is the ship’s transmitted text. Times are UTC.
