A little practice,
connected to school.

These activities practise selected skills from Ontario’s curriculum. The links below show the relevant expectations, what the app supports, and what still needs books, conversation, hands-on work or classroom lessons.

This is a practice companion, with partial curriculum coverage. Card visits and game completions show practice, not achievement of an Ontario expectation. The school popcorn words are the supplied classroom list; Ontario does not prescribe this particular list.

Grade 1 · Build the foundations

FOUNDATIONAL PRACTICE

Letters, sounds & words

Alphabet pictures, letter sounds, word families, school popcorn words and the picture-word game support Language B2, foundations for reading and writing.

Say the sounds, blend them, and notice the tricky part of a heart word. Add reading in sentences, spelling and writing; picture recognition alone does not establish word-reading skill.

Ontario Grade 1 Language · B2 ↗
PARTIAL EXPECTATION PRACTICE

Numbers & counting

Number cards and ten-frames support Math B1.1 (representing numbers) and B1.5 (counting). The counting game practises quantities from 1 to 10.

Ontario’s Grade 1 range is up to 50. 51–100 is an optional stretch. Also practise zero, counting by 2s, 5s and 10s, comparing quantities and explaining groups.

Ontario Grade 1 Math · B1 ↗
PARTIAL EXPECTATION PRACTICE

Little sums

Make-ten, addition and subtraction cards practise part of Math B2.2: addition facts through 10 and their related subtraction facts. Dots show both the parts and the total.

Use counters and explain how an addition fact helps solve a related subtraction.

Ontario Grade 1 Math · B2.2 ↗
FOUNDATIONAL PRACTICE

Shapes & patterns

Shape names and features prepare for Math E1.1 sorting. Repeating patterns support C1.1–C1.3: describing, creating and extending patterns.

Cards introduce the ideas; sort real objects, build a pattern and explain its rule to practise the wider expectations.

Ontario Math · E1 ↗ · C1 ↗
PARTIAL EXPECTATION PRACTICE

Canadian coins

Real nickel, dime, quarter, loonie and toonie images support coin recognition within Math F1.1. The counting cards add simple applications.

The full expectation also includes other coins, bills up to $50 and comparing values. Images show designs, not coins at their actual or relative sizes.

Ontario Grade 1 Math · F1.1 ↗
FOUNDATIONAL PRACTICE

Days, months & seasons

These cards build the vocabulary for Math E2.3, using a calendar to understand dates and units of time.

Use a real calendar together to find today, the next weekend and a family occasion. Naming months is only a starting point.

Ontario Grade 1 Math · E2.3 ↗
PARTIAL EXPECTATION PRACTICE

Living things

Needs, plants and animals connect to Science B2.1–B2.3: living things, their environment and how their features help them meet their needs.

Add outdoor observation and discussion. Human body parts, environmental care and other Grade 1 science strands need further activities.

Grade 4 · Make connections

DIRECT SKILL PRACTICE

Multiplication & division

Math B2.2 calls for multiplication facts from 1 × 1 through 10 × 10 and related division facts. The table lessons, division cards and times-table matching game practise this skill.

Arrays and strategies help explain answers. Add word problems and multi-digit operations for the wider Number strand.

Ontario Grade 4 Math · B2.2 ↗
PARTIAL EXPECTATION PRACTICE

Fractions

Fraction bars and comparisons support parts of Math B1.4–B1.5. Equal-sized wholes help make fair comparisons.

Extend this with sharing problems, counting by fractions, the complete range of denominators and decimal tenths.

Ontario Grade 4 Math · B1 ↗
PRACTICE + REVIEW + STRETCH

Time & money

Elapsed-time questions support Math E2.3. Clock reading and coin counting are useful review.

F1.2 focuses on whole-dollar purchases and change. This app’s cents-based transactions are extra practice; payment methods, saving and budgeting need separate learning.

Ontario Math · E2.3 ↗ · F1 ↗
PARTIAL EXPECTATION PRACTICE

Canadian capitals

Ten provincial capitals and the matching game practise part of Social Studies B3.5.

The expectation also includes the three territories, their capitals, locations and peoples. Use a map and explore those next. World capitals are enrichment.

Ontario Grade 4 Social Studies · B3.5 ↗
ENRICHMENT

Canadian history

The Canada-through-time cards introduce important people and events. Ontario’s Grade 4 history strand is Early Societies to 1500 CE.

These cards do not cover that strand. Its study of early societies, including early First Nations and Inuit societies, needs separate lessons.

Ontario Social Studies topics by grade ↗
PARTIAL EXPECTATION PRACTICE

Science discoveries

Habitats, light and sound, simple machines, and rocks connect to Grade 4 Science B–E. The cards introduce selected concepts from those strands.

Investigations, design, environmental connections and explaining observations need hands-on work. The general science and space quiz also includes review and enrichment.

Ontario Grade 4 Science strands ↗
FOUNDATIONAL PRACTICE

Reading

Short passages with questions support a small part of Language C2, comprehension strategies.

Discuss evidence for an answer and read a wider range of texts. Three short passages do not establish Grade 4 reading level or cover writing, media literacy and oral communication.

Ontario Grade 4 Language · C2 ↗
FOUNDATIONAL PRACTICE

Basic French

Colours, numbers, greetings and familiar words offer introductory vocabulary. Core French is required from Grade 4 in Ontario’s English-language schools.

The full program develops listening, speaking, reading and writing. Use the words in real exchanges; vocabulary matching alone covers only a small part.

A few minutes together

Choose one small set. Ask your child to say what they notice and explain an answer. Return to familiar cards another day, and connect them to a book, a calendar, coins or objects at home. Let the classroom teacher’s sequence and your child’s readiness guide what comes next.
